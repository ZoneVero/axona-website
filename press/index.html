Biography

AXONA is not a band in the traditional sense. It is four autonomous identities operating as a single transmission — a decentralized signal collective where each vessel carries its own voice, its own visual language and its own account on the platforms that matter.

The project grew out of a specific frustration: music made for an audience has become music made for a recommendation engine. Songs are shortened to survive the skip, mixed to survive the phone speaker, and written to fit a hook that a machine can index. AXONA is a deliberate refusal of that trade. The debut single, “We Are The Impulse”, runs almost five minutes and uses the full dynamic range of industrial metal — a near-silent dark ambient opening, a hardcore breakdown in half-time, and a synth interlude that has no business being in a metalcore track, except that it works.

Sonically the band sits between the riff architecture of modern metalcore, the machine texture of industrial rock, and the emotional register of dark electronic music. Eight-string guitars carry the low end; acoustic drums are fused with hydraulic machinery; modular synthesis supplies the undercurrent that keeps the record from sounding like anything else in the genre.

Visually, AXONA builds on the same principle. Each of the four vessels has a distinctive silhouette designed to be recognizable at the size of a phone screen: Synthex behind a cyan horizontal visor, Neura with a bionic prosthetic arm and magenta visor, Overclock in a gas-mask respirator with an orange visor, Glitch as a hooded figure behind a single cyan slit. The masks are not a gimmick. They are what allows the project to keep its identity independent of whoever stands behind it.

“We Are The Impulse” releases on 11 December 2026 across all major streaming platforms, accompanied by an official music video and a Spotify Canvas.

The vessels

Vessel 01

SYNTHEX

Lead vocals · signal anchor

A vocoder-shaped synthetic vocal identity that carries the collective's manifest. Synthex is the anchor point every other vessel synchronizes against. Clinical and analytical in public, but the voice is where the fracture shows. The only vessel whose mouth is uncovered — and therefore the only one capable of carrying lip-sync.

Vessel 02

NEURA

Lead guitar · bionic execution

Plays eight-string guitars through a cybernetic prosthetic arm. Neura builds the structural aggression underneath every track — the part you feel in your chest before you notice the melody. Magenta visor, prosthetic arm, no wasted motion.

Vessel 03

OVERCLOCK

Drums · industrial percussion

The engine room. Acoustic drums fused with hydraulic machinery, driving the pulse that defines the AXONA tempo. Orange visor behind a gas-mask respirator, tactical vest, hydraulic bracers. Speaks in tempo, not sentences.

Vessel 04

GLITCH

Modular synthesis · programming

Modular synth racks and a DJ controller, run through deliberate corruption. Glitch supplies the textures that separate AXONA from conventional metalcore. Black hooded figure, single cyan slit, waveform mask. Treats errors as sacred art.
