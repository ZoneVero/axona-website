Who we are, how this works

Version 1.0 · 15 November 2026

AXONA is a character-based music project. Four roles, one voice, and a fixed visual code. The debut single “We Are The Impulse” releases on 11 December 2026.

The four members — SYNTHEX, NEURA, OVERCLOCK and GLITCH — are characters, not individuals. They are roles within the AXONA world, written, designed and produced as a single body of work. Each role has a fixed stage name, silhouette, accent colour, instrument and way of speaking.

SYNTHEX
Lead vocals · the voice on every record
NEURA
Lead guitar · prosthetic right arm
OVERCLOCK
Drums · the clock nobody sets
GLITCH
Modular synths · DJ

01How the music is made

The music is made with AI tools and a great deal of human decision: writing, arrangement, structure, lyrics, editing, mixing and the final judgement on everything that gets released. Nothing reaches the catalogue by accident.

Songwriting, production and vocal identity are fixed in the recordings. The voice you hear on the records is the voice. A performer interpreting a role does not redefine it.

We are not hiding this. The project is built on the machinery it sings about. Saying so is not a confession — it is the position.

02How the characters work

The characters are performed by authorised people, under agreement. No performer is announced, and no identity is confirmed or denied. The mask is the identity; the person behind it is not part of the work.

The full statement of what stays constant, what a performer brings, and how the roles are carried forward is published in the Performance Protocol.

03Correspondence

Email sent to AXONA addresses is drafted with automated tools and reviewed by a person before it is sent. Nothing leaves this project without a human reading it first.

If you write to one of the character addresses, the reply is written in the character’s voice — but it is still a human who approves it. The characters are not individuals and will never claim to be.

Anything involving money, rights, press, bookings or the law is answered by a person, not a character.

04What we ask of you

We read everything. We reply to what we can. If you wrote about something that matters, we will answer as a person, not as a character.

Fan work is welcome — cosplay, covers, edits, artwork made for yourself. Selling costumes, masks or replica props requires a written agreement, because the visual code belongs to everyone who builds one.

05Your data

What we collect, why, how long we keep it and how to have it removed is set out in the privacy policy. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

For anything related to your personal data, write to contact@weareaxona.com.
