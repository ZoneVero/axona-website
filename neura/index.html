Vessel 02

NEURA

LEAD GUITAR

I build the part you feel before you notice it.

Machinery doesn't get tired. That is its only advantage.

There is a moment in every heavy song where the floor drops out and your chest responds before your brain does. Nobody teaches that. It comes from a low frequency, tuned wrong on purpose, played through an arm I was not born with.

The arm is not a costume. It is the reason I can hold a chord for forty seconds without my hand closing.

You will not hear me in the mix the way you hear VEX. You will notice me when I stop.

My mask is white porcelain with one vertical crack, lit glacial blue. It is the only thing in this project that people draw by hand. I did not plan that.

Specifications

Instrument
Eight-string electric guitar, ambient textures
Voice
Second voice of the collective — not yet spent
Face
Matte white porcelain mask, single vertical luminous crack
Camera
Lives in the low end and in the gaps — wherever the frame has room to breathe
The mask is white because nothing here is hidden, only covered.
