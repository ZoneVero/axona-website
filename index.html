Frequently asked

Who is AXONA?

AXONA is a cyber-metalcore collective built around four autonomous characters — Synthex on vocals, Neura on guitar, Overclock on drums and Glitch on modular synthesis. The music combines eight-string guitars, industrial percussion and modular synthesis.

When does “We Are The Impulse” release?

11 December 2026, across all major streaming platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and TikTok.

Who are the members of AXONA?

Four: Synthex (lead vocals, signal anchor), Neura (lead guitar, bionic execution), Overclock (industrial percussion at 150 BPM) and Glitch (modular synthesizers, sound design).

What genre is AXONA?

Cyber-metalcore, sitting at the intersection of industrial metal, electronic rock and djent.

Who performs the characters live?

AXONA is a character-based performance project. Synthex, Neura, Overclock and Glitch are roles within the AXONA world, embodied by authorized performers. The characters and their visual codes remain continuous; the cast may evolve by territory, production and era.

Does the cast change affect the music?

No. The voice you hear on the records is the voice. Songwriting, production and vocal identity are fixed in the recordings. A live performer interprets a role — they do not redefine it.
