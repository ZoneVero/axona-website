// Performance Protocol

Roles, not people

Version 2.0 · 15 November 2026

AXONA is a character-based performance project. SYNTHEX, NEURA, OVERCLOCK and GLITCH are roles within the AXONA world, embodied by authorised performers. The characters and their visual codes remain continuous.

SYNTHEX is the voice. Who carries it doesn’t change what you hear.

Songwriting, production and vocal identity are fixed in the recordings. A performer interprets a role — they do not redefine it.

01What stays the same, always

These are the character. No production, territory or cast changes them.

  • The stage name — SYNTHEX, NEURA, OVERCLOCK, GLITCH
  • The silhouette and mask
  • The accent colour in the visual code
  • The vocal timbre on all recorded material
  • The instrument each role carries
  • The archetype — how the character reads the world
  • The linguistic hook — how the character speaks

02What a performer brings

A performer brings interpretation, never canon.

Adds to the role

  • Stage presence and physical timing
  • Reading the room and reacting to it
  • Improvised ad-libs within the written line
  • Mannerisms specific to a period

Never changes

  • Backstory facts
  • Relationships between characters
  • Base temperament
  • Vocal identity

03Who is behind the mask

No performer is announced. No identity is confirmed or denied. This is not evasion — it is the position every masked project that has lasted has taken: the mask is the identity, and the person behind it is not part of the work.

What you watch is the role. What you remember is the character.

04Fan work

Fan cosplay is welcome. Convention photos, personal content, costume builds for yourself — all encouraged, no permission needed.

Selling costumes, masks or replica props is not permitted without a written agreement. This protects the visual code for everyone who builds one.

05Fan feedback

Fans influence the character. They do not write it.

  • We read. Comments, emails and replies are reviewed and analysed for themes and which language lands.
  • We decide. The characters’ canon facts, personality and commercial terms are not decided by vote.
  • We say when we change our mind. When there is a real fork with two or three defensible directions, the options are published with their real cost, and the decision is published too — including when it goes against the majority.

06If you want to perform

AXONA is an open-role project. Roles are carried by agreement, not by audition tapes sent to a public inbox. If you have stage experience and want to be considered, write to contact@weareaxona.com with your background and the role you are interested in.
