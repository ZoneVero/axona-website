Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your personal data when you visit weareaxona.com or submit your email address through the pre-save form. It is written to be readable, not to hide behind legal language.

In short: the only personal data we collect is the email address you choose to type in. We use it to tell you about AXONA releases. We do not sell it, rent it, or trade it. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing contact@weareaxona.com.

01  Who is responsible

The data controller for this website is the operator of AXONA, reachable at contact@weareaxona.com. For any question about this policy or about your data, that address is the one to use.

02  What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address Only if you submit the form in section // 04 "Intercept the master signal" To send you release announcements and occasional project updates
Technical logs Automatically, on every visit Our hosting provider records IP address, browser type and requested pages for security and abuse prevention

We do not collect names, phone numbers, postal addresses or payment details. There is no account system and no login.

03  Legal basis

For the email address you submit, the legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you press "Decode". You may withdraw consent at any time by asking to be removed from the list.

For technical logs kept by our hosting and infrastructure providers, the legal basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in keeping the site online and secure.

04  Who processes data for us

We keep the number of service providers deliberately small. These are the ones that may touch data submitted through this site:

  • Google — the email form writes to a Google Apps Script endpoint, which stores submissions in a Google Sheet. Google acts as a data processor and may process data outside the EEA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Cloudflare — the site is served through Cloudflare, which processes request data to deliver pages and protect against attacks.
  • DistroKid — the pre-save and "listen" links point to DistroKid's HyperFollow pages. Once you leave weareaxona.com, DistroKid's own privacy policy applies.

We do not run advertising networks, tracking pixels, retargeting scripts or third-party analytics on this site. There are no cookies set by us for advertising purposes.

05  Embedded media

Video content on this site is self-hosted and served from our own domain, so watching it does not report anything to a third party.

The social links in the footer (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Bluesky) are ordinary links. Nothing is loaded from those platforms until you click them, at which point their own privacy policies apply.

06  How long we keep it

Your email address stays on the list until you ask us to remove it, or until the project goes dormant and the list is deleted. Whichever comes first. Deletion requests are actioned within 30 days.

Server logs held by our infrastructure providers are retained on their standard short-term schedules, typically measured in days to weeks.

07  Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted ("right to be forgotten");
  • withdraw your consent and be removed from the mailing list;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email contact@weareaxona.com. We will respond within one month, as required by law.

08  Children

This site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has submitted an email address, contact us and we will delete it.

09  Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the submitted email list is restricted to the project operator. No system is perfectly secure, but the data at risk here is limited to email addresses — we hold nothing else about you.

10  Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — for example if we add a newsletter provider or start using analytics — the "last updated" date at the top will change and the substance of the change will be described here. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11  Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else about your data: contact@weareaxona.com.
